#  HealthGen AI

**HealthGen AI** is an intelligent healthcare application that uses Generative AI concepts to assist in organizing patient information and generating structured medical reports.

The project aims to provide a simple and user-friendly platform where healthcare-related information can be entered, managed, and transformed into clear and organized reports.

##  Key Features

*  Patient information management
*  Patient ID-based record handling
*  Hospital search and selection
*  Patient symptom and medical history entry
*  AI-assisted medical report generation
*  Structured and easy-to-read reports
*  Save generated reports
*  Download reports
*  Share reports
*  Report history and management
*  User-friendly notifications
*  Clean and modern healthcare interface
*  Responsive design for different devices

##  Objective

The main objective of HealthGen AI is to demonstrate how Generative AI can assist in organizing healthcare information and producing structured reports from provided patient data.

## Vision

HealthGen AI aims to make healthcare information management more organized, accessible, and efficient by combining modern technology with AI-assisted report generation.

 ## Generative AI

The project is designed to use Generative AI for producing meaningful, structured report content from the patient information and symptoms provided by the user.

## Applications

HealthGen AI can be useful as a concept for:

* Healthcare information management
* Medical report organization
* Patient record assistance
* Healthcare technology demonstrations
* AI-based healthcare research and learning
